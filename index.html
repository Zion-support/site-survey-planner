What this page provides

A human-reviewed planning checklist and related free tools. It does not connect to ERP or CRM, analyze data with AI, optimize routes, book technicians or deploy integrations.

Before the survey

  1. Define the site, survey objective, owner and approved access window.
  2. Confirm authorization, local contact, safety constraints and required equipment.
  3. Record the baseline and measurements needed for the decision.
  4. Agree which photos and notes may be collected; keep confidential client data out of public tools.
  5. Define the human-review gate, success metric and stop rule.
  6. Review findings with the responsible team before any dispatch, purchase or implementation.
