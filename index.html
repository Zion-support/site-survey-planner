Site Survey Planner

Prepare a clear, human-reviewed survey scope before visiting a site or connecting a tool to operational systems. This page provides a planning checklist and links to free tools; it does not connect to ERP, CRM or ticketing, analyze data with AI, optimize routes, book technicians or perform a deployment.

Before the survey

  1. Define the site, survey objective, process owner and approved access window.
  2. Confirm authorization, local contact, safety constraints and any required equipment.
  3. Record the existing baseline and the measurements needed for a useful decision.
  4. Agree which photos and notes may be collected; do not upload confidential client data to public tools.
  5. Specify the human-review gate, success metric and conditions for stopping the survey.
  6. Review findings with the responsible team before any dispatch, purchase or implementation.
