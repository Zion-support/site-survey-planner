# Site Survey Planner translation contract

Five equivalent static pages are published: [English](https://ziontechgroup.com/site-survey-planner/), [Português](https://ziontechgroup.com/site-survey-planner/pt/), [Español](https://ziontechgroup.com/site-survey-planner/es/), [Français](https://ziontechgroup.com/site-survey-planner/fr/), [Deutsch](https://ziontechgroup.com/site-survey-planner/de/).

Scope, all six checklist items, headings, descriptions, help, delivery caveat and navigation labels are translated. Product names are retained as brands. Each page has a unique canonical, five reciprocal hreflang links plus x-default, a shared stylesheet, skip/main landmarks and locale-preserving Discovery/brief links. All pages work without JavaScript.

This is a planning guide, not an AI integration, safety certification or dispatch booking system. The same limitation must remain in every translation. Update all five pages together when changing the contract; run `python3 scripts/test-locales.py` before publication. CI checks the same contract automatically.

The new localized planning-directory paths /pt/apps/, /es/apps/, /fr/apps/ and /de/apps/ are published by the main website, not this repository. These directory routes are a curated planning-tool selection, not a complete translation of the historical network. Related app interfaces may have a different language. Verify deployed language/canonical/link markers and avoid claiming full-network translation is done.
